The app does not request any permissions. Onboarding does not ask for an account or a permission, and no usage-description strings appear.

There is no login, no in-app purchase, no user-generated content, no account deletion flow, and no App Tracking Transparency prompt. Meals are bundled or taken from TheMealDB; the person does not write recipes.

Galleyweave is a cook-along for two dishes at once. A person saves meals, places one on Main and one on Second, then follows a single interleaved line of checks and steps until both dishes are done. It is for someone cooking a pair of recipes on one night, not for shopping lists or calorie tracking.

No credentials or demo account are needed. A cold launch shows a system launch screen, then three onboarding pages, each with Skip at the top. Skip opens the empty kitchen. Page one is titled Two dishes, one line. with body Save a meal, then fill the open place. and button Continue. Page two is titled Follow the lit step. with body Mark it done. Move to the other dish when it needs you. and button Next. Page three is titled Finish the night. with body When both dishes are done, the pair stays on this device. and button START. Continue and Next advance; START opens the kitchen.

The empty kitchen shows The kitchen is clear. and Fill a main and a second dish. Tap Place to open Saved meals. On a new device this is Nothing is saved yet. even though four meals sit on the Search shelf. Tap Search, Save a meal such as Herb chicken, Close, then tap that row (Place) to seat Main. Place again to seat Lemon greens on Second. When the chip is Seated, The line lists every Check and Step. Start stays dim until then. Tap Start. The chip is Lit. A step rail of beads replaces The line. Done finishes the lit check or untimed step. Undo puts back the last Done or Other dish. When finished, Both dishes are served. and both places read Open. A Timing wait does not come back after leaving the app; tap Done again to start that wait.

Saved meals, Search, and Settings open over the kitchen; Close returns. The gear is Settings. Place is disabled once both places are filled, so Saved meals and Search cannot be opened from the kitchen while both dishes are seated or lit. Search opens only from Saved meals.
